Privacy Policy — Monster Madness 3 Global Leaderboard
Last updated: October 25, 2026
This policy covers the optional global leaderboard in Monster Madness 3. Local play and the local leaderboard do not send any data anywhere and are not covered here, because nothing leaves your device.
What is collected
Only if you choose to sign in with Google and post to the global leaderboard:
Your Google account identifier (a numeric ID, used only to link your scores to your account).
Your display name for the board (taken from your Google profile name; you can change the board name in-game).
Your email address (as provided by Google; used only to identify your account).
Your scores, rounds cleared, and the replay data of a run (board states and random draws), which the server re-simulates to check the score is genuine.
A device identifier sent with submissions, used only for rate limiting and abuse prevention.
No passwords are ever collected or stored. Google handles sign-in; this game never sees your Google password.
What it is used for
Operating the global leaderboard: ranking scores, showing names on the board, and verifying runs.
Preventing cheating and spam: rate limits per account, and admin actions (removing scores, banning accounts that abuse the board).
What is not done
Scores and account data are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.
No payment information is collected.
No data is collected from players who never sign in.
Where it is stored
Leaderboard data is stored on Cloudflare's infrastructure (Workers + D1 database), which hosts the leaderboard service.
Deletion
To delete your leaderboard account and scores, open an issue on the game's GitHub repository (https://github.com/x0trouble/MonsterMadness3) asking for deletion, and your account, scores, and replay data will be removed.
Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here.
